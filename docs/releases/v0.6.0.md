Keeps niche AI recommendations visible and adds YouTube and SoundCloud listening, including recommendations for artists outside mainstream album catalogs.

- **All records** now retains suggestions that the music catalogs cannot confirm, clearly marked **Unverified**. Records without clips stay available to save, hide, or investigate. Owned and hidden releases remain excluded.
- Records without a catalog clip offer **Find on YouTube**. Search in your browser and paste a video link without a key, or add a separate encrypted **YouTube Data API v3** key in **Settings → YouTube** for in-app search.
- In-app searches run only when requested, check embedding and regional availability, and rank artist/title and artist-channel matches first. Choose the recording yourself; a video match does not verify an AI suggestion.
- Playback uses YouTube's visible video player. Catalog audio pauses when it opens; closing, hiding, or minimizing stops the video. Restore the app and choose **Reload video** to load it again without autoplay. Browser links remain available for restricted or unavailable videos.
- **With previews** and **Listen** still use catalog clips. Use **All records** for the full recommendation batch and YouTube fallback. Search caching reduces repeat API calls, and quota errors offer manual search and pasted links.
- **SoundCloud** adds an official waveform player for public track/playlist links, usable without app credentials. Saved records retain their chosen upload links. In-app search is available with separately encrypted SoundCloud client credentials; access and refresh tokens are reused and stored securely.
- AI recommendations can include **tracks and EPs**. Enable **Include SoundCloud discoveries** under Personalize & review to let the AI choose from actual public SoundCloud search results. Those picks retain their real upload identity and remain eligible without Apple, Deezer, or MusicBrainz matches. A confirmed upload does not prove the uploader's identity; manually associated links do not automatically verify a suggestion.
- Track ownership, hidden choices, and saved-record reconciliation distinguish tracks from albums and normalize common artist-prefixed upload titles. SoundCloud playback stops when its player is closed, hidden, or out of view, and resumes only after an explicit reload/play action.
- Album cards and details show catalog-backed track counts, including source/edition details and multi-disc totals. Older saved albums refresh their count when opened, and missing metadata can be retried. The matcher prefers an exact requested edition over deluxe/anniversary variants.
- Amazon lookup tries more catalog identifiers, explains failed mappings, and offers an explicit retry. Different mapped editions are offered separately with their track counts and Open/Run actions. An album existing in Amazon search does not guarantee that a sharing-service mapping exists.
- **Activity → Run a pasted URL** and record details can launch your configured external program directly with a supplied HTTP/HTTPS URL. `{url}` and `{amazonUrl}` are both supported, with literal argument handling. Pasted runs retain normal working-folder, output, Stop, and import-handoff behavior.

Library data, credentials, preferences, and music are preserved. YouTube playback may show ads and is subject to regional and embedding restrictions. No YouTube audio is extracted or saved as music files. Version 0.4.1 and newer can receive this update in the app after publication; older installations need one manual installer upgrade. This installer is unsigned.
