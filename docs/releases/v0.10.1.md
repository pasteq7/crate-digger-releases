Crate Digger 0.10.1 makes large collections more responsive.

- Fix repeated full metadata-table reads for tracks without personal details. Album opening and favorite edits now fetch only the needed track details.
- Move collection loading, filtering, sorting, grouping, and track resolution to a read-only background worker so they do not block the Electron main process.
- Cache decoded metadata and filtered results across pages. Database revisions invalidate the cache after scans, edits, imports, playlist changes, or folder recovery; playback-position and settings writes leave it intact.
- Start Library with a compact collection summary instead of transferring every track into the renderer. Load discovery and taste details when those pages need them.
- Debounce search, ignore stale responses, show retryable collection errors, and allow queue preparation to be canceled.
- Build filter controls on demand and limit the artist chooser's rendered options, with artist search for large lists.
- Limit concurrent artwork reads, share in-flight requests, and cap artwork cache memory.
- Render large album tracklists and playback queues in pages of 100 rows while retaining their full playback order.

Validated with an isolated catalog of 50,000 indexed tracks / 5,000 albums, representative generated audio, and 1,000-track album and queue cases. Existing music files remain read-only. The database, credentials, preferences, app identity, and update feed are preserved. The installer remains unsigned.
